Straight lines: with Road, Rail or Wire, click where the line starts, then hold Shift and click where it ends. If the end isn't in line, it turns one corner (an L shape).

Undo: Ctrl+Z (Windows) or Cmd+Z (Mac), or the Undo button. Undoes one click or drag at a time and refunds the money, as long as the city hasn't already changed those tiles.

Scroll the map: arrow keys or W A S D.
